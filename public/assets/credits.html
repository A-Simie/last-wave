Last Wave · Art and media credits

Tideline and Seafront Beach use the following licensed models, animation and footage. Thank you to their creators.

Café and beach models and characters · CC0 1.0

Released under CC0 1.0.

  • KayKit Furniture Bits and KayKit Restaurant Bits by Kay Lousberg: tables, chairs, shelves, books, picture frames, jars and menus.
  • Furniture Kit and Food Kit by Kenney: counters, cabinets, appliances, TV, plants, cups, pastries, plates and the paper bag.
  • Universal Base Characters and Universal Animation Library by Quaternius: bodies, faces, hair, locomotion/conversation, driving, pointing, falling, and beach swimming/treading-water clips.
  • Ceiling Fan by Ulan Cabanilla / Poly Haven.
  • Bottle and Ceiling Light by Quaternius.
  • Soda Glass by Kenney.

Last Wave prepares compact, self-contained GLBs, resizes textures, fits civilian clothing and swimwear, shapes body/facial variants, and binds selected animation while retaining bone lengths. The four beach variants reuse these CC0 character and animation sources with fitted, skinned swimwear. Everyday idle/walk and relaxed run clips bake gentler hand curl and upright idle/walk posture into the licensed tracks; driving adds a seated neck correction. The original files and preparation manifests are preserved in the project's asset library.

Porsche traffic model · CC BY-SA 4.0

(FREE) Porsche 911 Carrera 4S by Lionsharp Studios, obtained from the PlayCanvas Web Components examples. The original and Last Wave's traffic adaptation are licensed under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0.

Last Wave reduces geometry and textures, removes the display ground and duplicate lacquer shell, separates and straightens the four rolling wheels, fixes the brake calipers, and adapts the paint and glass materials. The source, attribution, preparation script and hashes are retained in the asset library.

Television footage and original voice

The Inside Story — Confronting the Climate Crisis, Episode 89 (April 27, 2023), presented by Elizabeth Lee. Credit: Voice of America / voanews.com. This excerpt contains VOA's own newsroom presentation, released into the public domain under its copyright terms.

The 15.4-second clip (programme 00:55.25–01:10.65) is resized to 960×540 and encoded as H.264/AAC. Captions follow the programme transcript, timed to the presenter's original voice. Last Wave's coastal bulletin, offshore mystery and TV graphics are fictional additions. Third-party agency footage is excluded.

Forest entrance · CC0

Trees, bare trees, rocks, grass and bushes: KayKit Forest Nature Pack by Kay Lousberg. Halloween pumpkins, lanterns, fences, candles, coffin, graves and skulls: Low Poly Halloween Asset Kit by Asset Quest. Forest ambience: Forest ambience 05 and forest footsteps by TuneSeeker. All three supplied packs include CC0 licenses, inspected November 10, 2026. Forest models were converted/calibrated and the KayKit palette desaturated for Last Wave. Bats, webs, leaves, phone, signboard and synthesized tension cues are original project artwork. The two unidentified figures reuse the credited Quaternius character rigs above.

Forest dialogue · synthetic speech

Original fictional dialogue is rendered offline with Hexgrad Kokoro 1.0 under Apache License 2.0, using am_michael for the man and af_bella for the woman. Generation uses kokoro-onnx (MIT). Voice timings, model hashes and preparation records are preserved in the source library. This dialogue is synthetic; the café TV separately retains its credited original reporter recording.

Music and original artwork

Tideline Afternoon is an original, deterministically synthesized music loop for Last Wave. Seafront Afternoon is an original 24-second surf and distant-gull ambience loop. Neither uses third-party samples. The wave sequence adds original procedural rumble/wind, whistle and heartbeat sound. Café architecture, neighborhood façades, window illustration, phone, carried props, steering wheels, beach boardwalk, umbrellas, loungers, citrus cart, water/foam, wave, birds, evacuation path, signs, Halloween branding and cinematic UI are project-created artwork.
