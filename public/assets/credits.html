Released under CC0 1.0.

  • KayKit Furniture Bits and KayKit Restaurant Bits by Kay Lousberg: tables, chairs, shelves, books, picture frames, jars and menus.
  • Furniture Kit and Food Kit by Kenney: counters, cabinets, appliances, TV, plants, cups, pastries, plates and the paper bag.

Last Wave prepares compact, self-contained GLBs, resizes textures, fits civilian clothing and body proportions, shapes facial variants, and retargets selected animation while retaining bone lengths. The original files and preparation manifests are preserved in the project's asset library.

Television footage and original voice

The Inside Story — Confronting the Climate Crisis, Episode 89 (April 27, 2023), presented by Elizabeth Lee. Credit: Voice of America / voanews.com. This excerpt contains VOA's own newsroom presentation, released into the public domain under its copyright terms.

The 15.4-second clip (programme 00:55.25–01:10.65) is resized to 960×540 and encoded as H.264/AAC. Captions follow the programme transcript, timed to the presenter's original voice. Last Wave's coastal bulletin, offshore mystery and TV graphics are fictional additions. Third-party agency footage is excluded.

Music and original artwork

Tideline Afternoon is an original, deterministically synthesized music loop for Last Wave, with no third-party samples. Café architecture, neighborhood façades, window illustration, phone, street vehicles, sun umbrellas, signs, Halloween branding and cinematic UI are project-created artwork.
