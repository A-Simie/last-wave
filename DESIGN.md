# Last Wave visual direction

The existing game uses stylized low-poly scenery, cream Georgia headings, compact Arial controls, warm pumpkin light and deep blue forest shadows. Preserve the Halloween mystery tone and existing logo. Accounts remain disconnected; the local-play route must always work.

The forest entrance is a continuous scene. Two figures cross the supplied wooden bridge and leave. The camera peers into a deep hole, accelerates downward, settles among skulls, skeletons, pumpkins and lanterns, and watches a physical timber sign turn. The HTML sign-in form is projected onto its front face; it has no independent floating-card border, chains, slide-in or fake wood background. The scene owns timber, lighting, camera and projection. HTML owns labels, validation, focus, password visibility and feedback.

The intro starts without an invitation overlay. Attempt sound automatically; browser denial must not halt the movie. The existing sound control enables or mutes it. Captions carry dialogue; omit the chain/rumble stage direction. Keep skip/replay, explicit skip preferences, focus/pause handling and reduced-motion immediate access. Decorative elements stay outside keyboard order. Never imply that disconnected account actions succeeded.

Frame the sign for desktop and portrait screens. Keep focus rings and native form semantics. Short landscape screens and physical-device performance require separate acceptance; no frame-rate claim follows from headless checks.
